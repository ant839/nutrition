  • Frozen blueberries 120 g
  • Honey 6 tbsp

Method
  1. Blend the cottage cheeseBlend cottage cheese until smooth.
  2. Combine the base mixIn a large bowl or jug, combine oats, blended cottage cheese, protein powder, milk and honey. Mix thoroughly until the protein powder is fully dissolved and there are no oat clumps.

3-Day Fries Prep
Cold soak, parboil, dry — prep once, cook fresh each night. Covers 3 dinners at 300g potato/serve.
This is prep only — no standalone macros. Potato is already counted in the Dinner card (300g per serve).
Ingredients — Full Batch (3 servings)
  • Potato 900 g
Method
  1. CutCut all 900g potato into fry-shaped batons.
  2. RinseRinse under cold water to remove loose surface starch.
  3. SoakSubmerge fully in a container of fresh cold water, fridge, for at least 30 minutes.
  4. ParboilParboil in boiling water for 4-5 minutes, until the outside just starts to soften but the middle's still firm.
  5. Drain and steam-dryDrain, then let sit in the colander a few minutes to steam-dry and rough up the edges slightly — this roughing is what crisps later.
  6. Pat dryPat fully dry with paper towel or a tea towel. This step matters more than the soak for getting a crisp result.
  7. PortionSplit into 3 containers, 300g each. Don't salt before storing — salt draws out moisture and makes them limp by day 3.
  8. FridgeFridge, unsalted, up to 3 days.
  9. CookStraight from the fridge into the air fryer at 200°C, no oil, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp. Salt after cooking.
Pre-Training Banana Bread
Rice cooker method. Full-flavour loaf, not a protein bread. Batch of 12 slices, 1 slice/day.

Ingredients — Full Loaf (12 slices)
  • Ripe banana, mashed 420 g
  • Butter, softened 140 g
  • Brown sugar 180 g
  • Large eggs 3
  • Plain flour 300 g
  • Baking soda 6 g
  • Salt 2 g
  • Vanilla extract 1.5 tbsp
Method — Rice Cooker
  1. Grease the rice cooker bowlGrease the rice cooker bowl thoroughly with butter or oil.
  2. Cream butter and sugarCream butter and brown sugar together until light and fluffy.
  3. Add eggs, banana, vanillaBeat in eggs one at a time, then mix in mashed banana and vanilla.
  4. Fold in dry ingredientsFold in flour, baking soda and salt until just combined — don't overmix.
  5. Pour batter inPour the batter into the greased rice cooker bowl and spread level.
  6. Run first cook cycleSelect the standard "Cook" setting (not quick-cook or brown rice). Let it run through to completion — it'll click over to "Warm" on its own.
  7. Run second cook cycleCheck the centre with a skewer. It almost always needs a second full cycle — press "Cook" again and repeat.
  8. Rest and sliceOnce a skewer comes out clean, rest in the bowl 10 minutes, then carefully turn out onto a rack to cool fully before slicing into 12.
Rice cooker won't brown the top or crisp the base like an oven — texture is softer and steamier throughout, which is normal for this method. Buttery, sweet loaf built for taste over protein, banked as pre-training fuel for two-session days.
